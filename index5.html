Frequently Asked Questions

Can I call Fun Cleaning?+

Not at this time. Fun Cleaning currently does not accept phone calls. Please contact us by text message, WhatsApp, email or the online quote request form.

How do I request a quote?+

Complete the online form, send us a text message, send a WhatsApp message or email funcleaningyeg@gmail.com.

Do you provide the service directly?+

Fun Cleaning helps arrange service with local professional service providers. We review the request, help confirm availability and communicate the estimated price before booking.

Will I receive an exact price immediately?+

Some requests can be estimated from the initial details. Other requests may require photos or additional information. We confirm pricing before the booking is finalized.

Do you offer recurring services?+

Recurring options may be available for house cleaning, lawn mowing and snow removal, depending on location and provider availability.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve Edmonton and may be able to arrange service in nearby communities depending on local provider availability.

How do I cancel or reschedule?+

Please contact us as soon as possible by text, WhatsApp or email. Cancellation and rescheduling terms depend on the notice provided and the type of service.

Are the prices final?+

The prices shown on the website are starting estimates. We confirm the specific service details and estimated price before the booking is finalized.
