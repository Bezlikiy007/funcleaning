Frequently asked questions

Will I get an exact price right away?+

Some requests can be priced from the first details. Others need a photo or a few more details. Either way, we confirm the price in writing before anything is booked. The starting prices on this site are estimates.

How fast will you reply?+

We usually reply within about an hour by text, WhatsApp or email.

What if I'm not happy with the result?+

Tell us by text and we'll arrange a free redo with your provider.

How do I pay?+

We confirm how and when to pay in writing, together with your price, before the service is booked.

Do I have to pay a deposit?+

No. After you submit a request you can choose to pay a small refundable-toward-your-total deposit to lock in your spot faster, but it's optional.

Do you bring your own supplies?+

Supplies and equipment are handled by the provider. If you have a specific request, such as your own products, tell us in your quote request.

Do you provide the service directly?+

Fun Cleaning arranges your service through local professional providers and stays your point of contact from the first message to the finished job.

Do you offer recurring services?+

Regular house cleaning, lawn mowing and winter snow removal can often be arranged. Availability depends on your location and provider capacity.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve Edmonton and nearby communities such as St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Stony Plain, Beaumont, Leduc and Fort Saskatchewan. Availability depends on the service and the provider.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?+

Text us as early as you can and we'll work with you and your provider to find a new time.

Can I call Fun Cleaning?+

Not at this time. To keep every detail in writing, we work by text message, WhatsApp, email and the quote form. Text is the fastest way to reach us.
